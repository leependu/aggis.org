Aaron Goodman←Geographic Information System

Thanks for visiting my website. This site began as a GIS portfolio and now serves as the face of my professional life. I am most trained in GIS tools, but I have been grateful for opportunities to apply these skills in diverse contexts.

My graduate coursework in Digital Humanities has further informed my approach to GIS and cartography and, along with work experience in a special collections library, has illuminated the value of integrating these disciplines.

My skillset includes:

  • cartography
  • data analysis and visualization
  • web mapping
  • relational database management
  • batch processing and automation
  • archival processing and description
  • digital asset management
